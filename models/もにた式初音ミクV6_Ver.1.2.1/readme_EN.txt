When loading "PostAlphaEye.x" from the effect folder into MMD, the eyes will show through the hair like a standing image.
Setting Tr to 0.5 looks good.

Instructions
1. Load the model.
↓
2. Loading PostAlphaEye.x makes it transparent.
↓
3. Go to the EyeRT tab in the MME Effect Assignment.
↓
4. Select the Monita-style Hatsune Miku V6.pmx, go to File -> "Load settings by model unit", and apply Monita-style Hatsune Miku V6_EyeRT.emd.

While step 2 alone makes it transparent, performing step 4 removes the transparency depending on the angle, making it look natural.
